# University Portal - Vision

My name is  eya hamdaoui  and I am a student at Université Centrale. I want to build a university portal because I want to create something that is connected to my own experience as a student.

When I first started learning programming, I honestly did not know anything about coding or software development. This is one of my first real projects, so I wanted to choose something that I can understand from a student's point of view.

As a student, I saw how important it is to have one place where we can find our courses, grades, documents, and other university information. Sometimes students can get confused about where to find things,THE  information ARE   spread between papers, different files, Teachers and administrators can also have a lot of documents to manage, which can take time and sometimes cause mistakes.

At Université Centrale, I discovered e-learning and platforms that help students and teachers manage their courses and learning materials. This made me interested in platforms lik MYU  and Blackboard. I thought: why not try to build something similar by myself?

My idea is to create a university portal where students can log in and find their courses, grades, learning materials, and important information in one place. Teachers could manage their courses, upload documents, and enter grades, while administrators could manage students, teachers, courses, and other university information.

I know that this is a big project for my current level, especially because I am still learning programming. But this is exactly why I want to do it. I don't want to only learn programming through small exercises. I want to learn by building a real project step by step.

For me, this project is not just a university portal. It is also my first real step into programming and software development.

## Users

- Student: sees their own courses and grades.
- Teacher: enters grades for the courses they teach.
- Admin: manages the university data (courses, teachers, students).

## MVP scope

The first version will let users do these things:

1. Log in with an email and a password.
2. The admin creates departments and courses.
3. The admin creates teacher and student accounts.
4. The admin enrolls students in courses.
5. The teacher enters grades for their students.
6. The student sees only their own courses and grades.

## Out of scope for now

I will not build these in the first version:

- Attendance
- Assignments and file uploads
- Notifications
- Timetable
- Statistics and activity logs

I will add them later, step by step.
